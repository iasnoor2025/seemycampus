## 🔍 Key Metrics to Track

### In Google Search Console

**Performance Tab:**
- **Total Clicks**: Organic traffic from Google
- **Total Impressions**: How often pages appear
- **Average CTR**: Click-through rate
- **Average Position**: Your ranking (lower = better)

**Pages Tab:**
- **Indexed Pages**: How many are in Google's index
- **Not Indexed**: Pages with issues
- **Errors**: Any indexing problems

**Enhancements Tab:**
- **FAQ**: Pages with FAQ structured data
- **Breadcrumbs**: Pages with breadcrumb markup
- **Reviews**: (When you add reviews)
