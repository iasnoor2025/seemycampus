# Project Brief: SeeMyCampus

## Overview
SeeMyCampus is an AI-powered admissions counseling platform designed to help Indian students find their perfect college match. The platform simplifies the college selection process for students pursuing undergraduate (UG) and postgraduate (PG) courses.

## Core Mission
Provide a holistic, comprehensive platform offering reliable and genuine information on over 60,000 institutions and 375,000+ courses, specifically catering to UG/PG students across popular educational streams.

## Target Audience
- Indian students pursuing undergraduate (UG) courses
- Indian students pursuing postgraduate (PG) courses
- Students interested in: MBA/BBA, Engineering, Medical, Law, Design, and more

## Key Value Propositions
1. **Comprehensive Database**: Access to 60,000+ institutions and 375,000+ courses
2. **AI-Powered Recommendations**: Personalized college matching based on student preferences
3. **Expert Guidance**: AI chatbot and counseling services
4. **Streamlined Process**: Simplified college discovery and application process

## Success Metrics
- Student engagement and lead generation
- College recommendation accuracy
- User satisfaction and conversion rates
- SEO performance and organic traffic growth
